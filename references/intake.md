# Travel intake

Use this question bank progressively. Ask no more than three questions at once and skip anything already answered.

## Round 1: route-shaping facts

1. What are the exact arrival and departure dates, local times, and airports or stations?
2. Who is traveling? Include adults, children’s ages, mobility needs, pregnancy, medical needs, and driver count.
3. Which places are must-do, optional, or explicitly excluded?
4. Which pace feels right?
   - Relaxed: one main anchor per day, frequent two-night bases.
   - Balanced: one to two anchors, moderate transfers.
   - Full: more stops and earlier starts, with explicit recovery periods.
5. What is the acceptable ordinary and absolute-maximum daily driving or transfer time?
6. Are there fixed sleep, nap, meal, medication, check-in, prayer, or bedtime windows?

## Round 2: execution preferences

1. Transport: rental car, private driver, rail, ferry, flights, public transport, walking, stroller, or cycling?
2. Lodging: already booked, preferred areas, nightly budget, room setup, parking, kitchen, laundry, or early check-in needs?
3. Interests: nature, beaches, wildlife, museums, food, photography, playgrounds, shopping, nightlife, or indoor backups?
4. Booking tolerance: willing to prepay, free-cancellation only, or avoid fixed-time tickets?
5. Output: language, map/text pages, images, downloadable file, print layout, and hosting preference?
